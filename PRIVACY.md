# Tassel Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

Tassel is a Mac app that shows a student's Canvas, StudentVUE and linked Google files in one place. This policy covers
what Tassel does with your information, including data from your Google account.

## Where your data lives

Everything Tassel collects stays on your Mac, in `~/Library/Application Support/Tassel` and your Mac's Keychain. There
is no Tassel server. Tassel's developer never receives, sees or stores your data, and Tassel has no ads, analytics or
tracking.

## What Tassel accesses

- **Canvas and StudentVUE:** your courses, assignments, grades, messages and schedule. Tassel gets these directly from
  your school's servers using the sign-in you give it.
- **Google (optional):** if you connect a Google account, Tassel uses Google's Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides access to
  read files your teachers link, show them in the app, search them, and create or edit files when you ask it to. It
  also reads your Google account's email address so it can tell your accounts apart.

## How Google data is used

Tassel uses Google user data only to provide these features to you, inside the app on your Mac. Tassel does not sell
Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. People don't read your
Google data, except when you ask for help and share it yourself.

Tassel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements.

## The optional assistant

If you turn on the assistant and choose Claude, the parts of your data needed to answer your request (for example, an
assignment or a linked document) are sent to Anthropic through your own Claude account, under
[Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy). If you choose Apple Intelligence, the assistant
runs on your Mac. Nothing is sent unless you use the assistant.

## Removing your data

Remove an account in **Settings → Accounts** to delete its data from Tassel. Deleting
`~/Library/Application Support/Tassel` removes everything. You can also revoke Tassel's Google access at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
